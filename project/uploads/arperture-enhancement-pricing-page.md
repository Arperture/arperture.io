## FAQ

**Can you really colorize black-and-white photos and video?**
Yes — and it's one of the things we do best. We research period-accurate tones so the result looks natural and historically plausible, not like a filter slapped on top.

**How much resolution can you add?**
We can upscale images up to 6× and video up to 4K (8K on request), rebuilding real detail rather than just stretching pixels.

**What if my source file is really bad?**
Send it anyway. We'll tell you honestly whether it's worth doing and what tier it needs. Some things AI can't invent — but we'll be straight with you before you spend anything.

**Do you offer bulk or ongoing rates?**
Yes. Whole-archive projects, event batches, and recurring content work all get custom pricing — just ask.

**How fast can I get it back?**
Standard turnaround is a few business days depending on volume. Need it in 24–48 hours? Add rush delivery.

## BUILDER NOTES (do not publish — for Claude Design layout)

- **Tier visual system:** Refresh / Restore / Remaster is the spine of the page. Use three cards side-by-side for images, three for video. Make **Restore** the visually highlighted / "most popular" middle card in each set — it's the anchor tier most buyers land on.
- **Two table sets, one system:** the shared Refresh/Restore/Remaster naming lets you reuse the exact same card component and color treatment for both Images and Video sections. Only the contents change.
- **Anchor high, sell the middle:** show Remaster prices openly — they make Restore feel like the reasonable choice. Classic price-anchoring.
- **Quote-gate the messy jobs:** every tier should route degraded/long source through "Get a Quote" rather than instant checkout. Publish package prices to anchor value; gate the outliers so you never underbid blind.
- **Retrofuturist / ANALOG-FUTURE fit:** the film-lab framing (Refresh/Restore/Remaster, "bring it back," "remaster") is deliberately on-brand for Arperture — lean into darkroom/analog texture, grain-to-clarity before/after sliders, and a "signal restored" motif.
- **Highest-converting element to add:** a before/after slider in the hero. Nothing sells restoration like watching it happen.
