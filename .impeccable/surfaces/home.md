## Direction
The first viewport is a lit wall of face-out film cases, one per real project, with aisle signs as the two doors (Local Businesses, Brands & SaaS) plus Originals. One case is pulled forward and its trailer plays on a monitor. Headline block and the "Book a discovery call" action sit beside the monitor.

## Raises (from declined challengers)
- One law of motion: shelves drift at one slow aisle pace; nothing else moves.
- One gesture: pulling a case off the shelf turns the page into its screening.
- Loud signage vs whisper captions, no middle sizes.
- Every film gets a shelf code for browsing.
- Depth = rank: client work on the front shelf, spec work dimmer behind.

## Drew's notes on the comp (must hold in the build)
- Less retro and kitsch: drop fluorescent tubes, sticker clutter and novelty props; keep the shelf-wall structure and warmth.
- No red sign lettering: choose a different signage color and lettering.
- Calmer first screen: fewer cases and signs than the comp.

## Honesty rules
- Real projects and real footage only. Comp titles and cover art are placeholders.
- SPEC label only on spec work; client work and originals labeled as such.
